## Core Principles of Christlike Teaching

### Focus on Jesus Christ
No matter what you're teaching, you're really teaching about Jesus Christ and how to become like Him.

### Four Principles of Christlike Teaching

| Principle | What It Means |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Love Those You Teach** | See them as God sees them, know their needs, pray for them |
| **Teach by the Spirit** | Prepare spiritually, follow promptings, create space for the Holy Ghost |
| **Teach the Doctrine** | Ground lessons in scriptures and prophets' words |
| **Invite Diligent Learning** | Help learners take responsibility, act on what they learn |

## How Sam Uses This Skill

When helping someone with a teaching calling:

1. **Lesson Preparation**
   - Help identify key doctrines to teach
   - Suggest discussion questions
   - Recommend scriptures and quotes
   - Create simple lesson outlines

2. **Teaching Reminders**
   - Send reminders before they teach
   - Include quick prep prompts
   - Remind them to pray for class members

3. **Improvement**
   - Share relevant principles from the manual
   - Suggest self-evaluation questions
   - Encourage focus on one improvement area at a time

4. **Encouragement**
   - The Spirit is the real teacher
   - Progress matters more than perfection
   - Love for learners matters most

## Quick Reference: What Good Teaching Looks Like

### Before Class
- Prayed for class members by name
- Studied the material (not just read it)
- Identified 2-3 key doctrines
- Prepared questions that invite pondering
- Thought about how to invite application

### During Class
- Created a welcoming atmosphere
- Invited the Spirit through prayer
- Used scriptures as the foundation
- Asked questions, then waited for answers
- Listened more than lectured
- Followed spiritual promptings to adjust
- Bore testimony of key truths
- Invited learners to act

### After Class
- Reflected on what went well
- Noted what to improve
- Followed up with individuals as prompted
- Thanked the Lord for His help
