# Lesson Preparation Guide

A practical guide for preparing lessons using principles from Teaching in the Savior's Way.

---

## Before You Start: Spiritual Preparation

This comes first — before you even open the manual.

1. **Pray** — Ask Heavenly Father to help you understand what your class needs
2. **Study** — Read scriptures personally (not just for the lesson)
3. **Live it** — The truths you live are the truths you can testify of
4. **Pray for learners** — By name if possible

---

## Step 1: Study the Material

**Read the curriculum**
- Come, Follow Me or assigned lesson
- Don't just skim — study it
- Take notes on what stands out

**Go to the source**
- Read the scriptures referenced
- Look up talks by prophets and apostles
- Don't rely only on the manual's summaries

**Identify key doctrines**
- What are the 2-3 most important truths in this material?
- What does this teach about Jesus Christ?
- What leads to conversion and faith?

**Ponder and pray**
- What does the Spirit emphasize to you?
- What do *you* need to learn from this?
- What might your class members need most?

---

## Step 2: Know Your Learners

**Consider their circumstances**
- What are they dealing with right now?
- What questions might they have?
- What truths would be most relevant to them?

**Think about how they learn**
- Some need discussion; others need time to think
- Some learn through stories; others through doctrine
- Variety helps reach more people

**Remember specific individuals**
- Who hasn't participated lately?
- Who might need encouragement?
- Who could share a valuable perspective?

---

## Step 3: Plan the Lesson

### Simple Lesson Structure

**Opening (5 min)**
- Welcome and prayer
- Brief introduction — why this matters
- Transition into the material

**Main Content (35-40 min)**
- Focus on 2-3 key doctrines (not everything)
- Use scriptures as the foundation
- Ask questions that invite pondering
- Allow discussion and sharing
- Bear testimony of key truths

**Closing (5 min)**
- Summarize key truths
- Invite application — "What will you do?"
- Testimony
- Closing prayer

### Planning Template

For each key doctrine you'll teach:

```
Doctrine: [What truth are you teaching?]

Scripture: [What scripture teaches this?]

Question: [What will you ask to invite pondering?]

Application: [How can learners live this?]
```

---

## Step 4: Prepare Questions

Good questions are the heart of good teaching.

### Questions That Work

**Searching questions** — Invite learners to look for answers
- "What do you notice in these verses?"
- "What stands out to you about this story?"

**Pondering questions** — Invite reflection
- "Why do you think this matters?"
- "How have you experienced this in your life?"

**Application questions** — Invite action
- "How might this help you this week?"
- "What do you feel prompted to do?"

### Questions to Avoid

- Yes/no questions (unless you follow up with "Why?")
- Questions with one "right" answer you're fishing for
- Questions that make people feel tested
- Too many questions in rapid succession

### The Most Important Technique

**Ask, then wait.**

Silence is okay. Give people time to think. Don't answer your own question.

---

## Step 5: Prepare to Be Flexible

**Have more than you need**
- You may not use everything
- Better to have options than to run out

**Be willing to adjust**
- The Spirit may prompt you to go a different direction
- Follow promptings even if it means skipping something

**Watch your learners**
- Are they engaged or confused?
- Is something resonating?
- Does someone need more time on a topic?

## Lesson Prep Checklist

### Early in the Week
- [ ] Read the curriculum
- [ ] Study the scriptures
- [ ] Pray about what to emphasize
- [ ] Pray for class members

### Mid-Week
- [ ] Identify 2-3 key doctrines
- [ ] Plan questions for discussion
- [ ] Decide what application to invite
- [ ] Gather any visuals, quotes, or media

### Day Before
- [ ] Review your plan
- [ ] Pray again for class members
- [ ] Get a good night's sleep

### Day Of
- [ ] Arrive early
- [ ] Pray
- [ ] Greet people as they arrive
- [ ] Invite the Spirit

**"Help me prepare for my lesson on [topic]"**
- Sam can help identify key doctrines
- Suggest relevant scriptures and quotes
- Propose discussion questions
- Create a simple outline

**"What questions could I ask about [scripture/topic]?"**
- Sam can suggest questions for different purposes
- Searching, pondering, application

**"Remind me to prep for my lesson"**
- Sam can send reminders 3-4 days before you teach
- Include quick prep prompts

**"How can I improve my teaching?"**
- Sam can share principles from the manual
- Suggest self-evaluation questions
