# The Four Principles of Christlike Teaching

Source: Teaching in the Savior's Way, Part 2

---

## 1. Love Those You Teach

**The Foundation:** Everything the Savior did was motivated by love. When His love is in our hearts, we seek every way to help others learn of Christ and come unto Him.

### How to Love Those You Teach

**See them as God sees them**
- In unpolished fishermen, the Savior saw future Church leaders
- In every person you teach, He sees a son or daughter of God with limitless potential
- Don't judge by appearances — the Spirit can help you see what God sees

**Know them and their circumstances**
- Take interest in their lives
- Understand their backgrounds, talents, interests, needs
- Find out how they learn best
- The better you know someone, the better you can help them find meaning in the gospel

**Pray for them by name**
- Imagine how learners feel knowing their teacher prays for them
- Prayer changes how you feel about people and how you act toward them
- God answers the sincere prayers of a teacher who desires to help a learner

**Create a safe environment**
- Everyone should feel respected and valued
- Reach out to those who don't attend or seem uninterested
- Help all feel not only welcome but *needed*
- Be patient if progress seems slow

**Express your love**
- Find appropriate ways to show you care
- Interact before and after class
- Listen intently
- Be willing to adjust plans to focus on what matters to learners

---

## 2. Teach by the Spirit

**The Foundation:** The Holy Ghost is the true teacher. You are not alone — "it is not ye that speak, but the Holy Ghost."

### How to Teach by the Spirit

**Prepare yourself spiritually**
- Spiritual preparation begins long before the lesson
- Invite the Holy Ghost into your life, not just your teaching
- Live the gospel with all your heart
- Repent daily

**Be ready to respond to promptings**
- Don't rush past urgent needs to "cover the material"
- Seek the Spirit's guidance while teaching, not just while preparing
- Be willing to alter your plans
- Spend more time on what matters most to learners now

**Create opportunities for the Spirit to teach**
- The learning environment matters (room arrangement, how you greet people)
- Invite learners to prepare spiritually too
- Provide moments for quiet pondering
- Use sacred music and art

**Help learners seek and recognize revelation**
- We all need help recognizing the Spirit's voice
- Ask questions that invite personal searching
- Help learners discover how the Spirit communicates with *them*
- This is one of the greatest gifts you can give

**Bear testimony often**
- Share what you know by the power of the Holy Ghost
- It doesn't need to be eloquent or lengthy
- Direct and heartfelt testimony is most powerful
- Invite learners to share their testimonies too

## 3. Teach the Doctrine

**The Foundation:** Jesus's teachings were powerful because He taught the Father's doctrine — eternal truth found in scriptures and prophets' words.

### How to Teach the Doctrine

**Learn the doctrine yourself first**
- Search scriptures and words of living prophets
- Living and applying truths invites the Spirit to teach you more deeply
- Don't just prepare a lesson — understand the doctrine

**Teach from the scriptures**
- The Savior used scriptures to teach, correct, and inspire
- Don't drift away from God's word
- Your love for scriptures will be evident and invite the Spirit

**Help learners find and understand truth**
- Stop and ask what truths they notice
- Explore historical background and meaning
- Help them see how truths relate to the plan of salvation
- Truth is sometimes stated; sometimes illustrated in stories

**Focus on truths that lead to conversion**
- Simple, basic truths have the greatest power to change lives
- The Atonement, the plan of salvation, love God and neighbor
- Don't get distracted by less essential topics
- Invite the Spirit to witness of these truths

**Help learners find personal relevance**
- Some learners don't see why truths matter to them
- Ask: "How can this help you?" "Why is this important to know?"
- Listen and allow questions
- Share how you've found relevance in your own life

---

## 4. Invite Diligent Learning

**The Foundation:** Discipleship requires faith and effort. The Savior invited followers to *experience* truths for themselves — "Come and see."

### How to Invite Diligent Learning

**Help learners take responsibility**
- Don't just tell them what to know
- Invite them to ask questions, search, ponder, share
- Let them discover truths themselves
- "I have learned for myself" (Joseph Smith)

**Encourage daily scripture study**
- Daily immersion in God's word is crucial for spiritual survival
- Help learners see scripture study as hearing the Lord's voice
- This is foundational to lifetime gospel learning

**Invite learners to prepare**
- Spiritual preparation matters for learners too
- Pray to find out what the Lord wants you to learn
- Nurture desire by asking sincere questions
- Open your heart to whatever He will teach

**Encourage sharing**
- Give learners opportunities to express what they're learning
- This helps internalize truths and builds confidence
- Creates powerful experiences for all

**Invite action**
- Don't hesitate to invite learners to *live* what they learn
- "What do you feel impressed to do?"
- Specific invitations when appropriate
- Living truth is the quickest path to testimony

---

## Summary: To Teach Like the Savior

| Principle | Key Actions |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Love** | See potential, know them, pray for them, create safety, express love |
| **Spirit** | Prepare spiritually, follow promptings, create space for revelation, testify |
| **Doctrine** | Learn it, teach from scriptures, help find truth, focus on conversion, make relevant |
| **Learning** | Let them take responsibility, encourage study, invite preparation, encourage sharing, invite action |
