# Teacher Council Meetings

How to run effective teacher council meetings to strengthen all gospel teachers.

Source: Teaching in the Savior's Way, "Teacher Council Meetings"

---

## Purpose

Teacher council meetings help teachers:
- Improve their teaching abilities
- Learn from each other
- Share ideas and experiences
- Address common challenges
- Feel supported and encouraged

---

## Who Should Attend

- All teachers in the ward (not just Sunday School)
- Primary teachers
- Youth advisors/teachers
- Elders Quorum instructors
- Relief Society instructors
- Parents (they're teachers too!)
- New teachers especially benefit

---

## Frequency

- Quarterly is typical
- Some wards do monthly
- Can be combined with 5th Sunday lessons
- Can be part of ward conference

---

## Who Leads

Usually the Sunday School president, but could be:
- A counselor in the Sunday School presidency
- A skilled teacher in the ward
- Rotating among experienced teachers

---

## Meeting Structure

### Suggested Format (45-60 minutes)

**Opening (5 min)**
- Prayer
- Welcome and purpose

**Principle Focus (25-30 min)**
- Teach ONE principle of Christlike teaching
- Use Teaching in the Savior's Way as the resource
- Include discussion — model good teaching

**Practice/Application (15-20 min)**
- Practice the principle
- Share ideas and experiences
- Role-play or demonstrations
- Small group discussions

**Closing (5 min)**
- Invite teachers to apply what they learned
- Express appreciation
- Closing prayer

---

## Topic Ideas

Each meeting, focus on ONE principle or skill:

### From Teaching in the Savior's Way
- Love those you teach
- Teach by the Spirit
- Teach the doctrine
- Invite diligent learning
- Asking good questions
- Using the scriptures
- Bearing testimony
- Inviting application

### Practical Skills
- How to handle difficult questions
- Engaging quiet learners
- Managing class discussions
- Teaching children
- Teaching youth
- Using technology effectively
- Preparing lessons efficiently
- Creating a welcoming environment

### Seasonal Topics
- Beginning of year: Setting the tone
- Before youth conferences: Teaching youth
- Before Primary program: Engaging children
- End of year: Review and refresh

---

## Making It Effective

### Keep It Practical
- Focus on skills teachers can use next Sunday
- Include demonstrations and practice
- Share specific examples

### Model Good Teaching
- The meeting itself should demonstrate good teaching
- Ask questions instead of just lecturing
- Invite participation
- Bear testimony

### Create Safety
- Teachers should feel comfortable sharing struggles
- No criticism or judgment
- Celebrate effort, not just success
- Newer teachers learn from experienced ones

### Make It Worth Attending
- Start and end on time
- Be organized
- Make it encouraging, not overwhelming
- Teachers should leave feeling equipped and uplifted

## Sample Meeting Plan

**Topic:** Asking Good Questions

**Opening (5 min)**
- Prayer
- "Today we're going to focus on one powerful skill: asking questions that invite the Spirit"

**Principle (15 min)**
- Read together from Teaching in the Savior's Way (section on inviting learning)
- Discuss: "Why are questions so important in teaching?"
- Share examples of good vs. poor questions

**Practice (20 min)**
- In pairs: Practice turning a "yes/no" question into an open question
- In groups of 3-4: Look at a scripture passage and create:
  - A searching question
  - A pondering question
  - An application question
- Share with the whole group

**Application (10 min)**
- "This week, try asking at least one question where you wait 10 seconds before anyone speaks"
- Testimony about how the Spirit teaches through questions

**Closing (5 min)**
- Appreciation for teachers
- Prayer

## For the Sunday School President

### Your Role
- Organize and oversee teacher council meetings
- Ensure all teachers feel supported
- Visit classes occasionally
- Coordinate training for new teachers

### Supporting Individual Teachers
- Check in periodically
- Offer encouragement
- Connect struggling teachers with experienced mentors
- Help teachers get resources they need

### New Teacher Orientation
When someone receives a new teaching calling:
1. Meet with them personally
2. Walk through Teaching in the Savior's Way basics
3. Connect them with curriculum resources
4. Invite them to teacher council
5. Check in after their first few lessons

---

## Remember

> "As you meet with teachers, consider ways to strengthen them and encourage them with kindness and gratitude for the service they give."
> — Teaching in the Savior's Way

Teacher council isn't about fixing bad teachers. It's about helping *all* teachers grow — including the experienced ones. Everyone can learn something.
