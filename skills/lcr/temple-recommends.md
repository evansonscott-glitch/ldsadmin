# LCR Temple Recommends

## Overview

LCR tracks temple recommend status for all ward members, helping leaders monitor expirations and coordinate renewal interviews.

## Access

**Path:** LCR → Temple

**Who can access:**
- Bishopric — Full access, can conduct interviews
- Executive Secretary — View access, scheduling
- Stake Presidency — Full access for second interviews
- Ward Clerk — View access

---

## Temple Recommend Types

### Limited-Use Recommend
- For members who haven't received their endowment
- Youth (12+) and adults preparing for endowment
- Valid for ordinances: baptisms for the dead, confirmations
- Valid for 2 years

### Regular Temple Recommend
- For endowed members
- Access to all temple ordinances
- Valid for 2 years
- Requires both bishopric and stake presidency interviews

---

## Viewing Recommend Status

### Ward Overview
**Path:** LCR → Temple → Recommend Status

Shows:
- Members with current recommends
- Expiring soon (30, 60, 90 days)
- Expired recommends
- Members without recommends

### Individual Member
**Path:** LCR → Membership → [Member] → Temple

Shows:
- Current recommend status
- Expiration date
- Previous recommend history

### Expiring Recommends Report
**Path:** LCR → Temple → Expiring Recommends

Filter by timeframe:
- Next 30 days
- Next 60 days
- Next 90 days

---

## Temple Recommend Interview Process

### Step 1: Schedule Interview
- Member requests renewal
- Or proactive outreach for expiring recommends
- Executive secretary schedules with bishopric member

### Step 2: Bishopric Interview
**Who conducts:** Bishop or counselor

**Interview includes:**
- Temple recommend questions (standardized)
- Discussion of worthiness
- If approved, bishopric member signs

### Step 3: Stake Presidency Interview
**Who conducts:** Stake president or counselor

**After bishopric interview:**
- Member schedules stake interview
- Stake presidency conducts second interview
- If approved, stake presidency member signs
- Recommend is activated

### Step 4: Record in LCR
After both interviews:
- Recommend is issued in LCR
- System tracks expiration

---

## Recommend Questions

The temple recommend interview uses standard questions approved by the First Presidency. These questions cover:
- Faith in God and Jesus Christ
- Sustaining Church leaders
- Living the law of chastity
- Honesty
- Word of Wisdom
- Tithing
- Family relationships
- Apostate groups
- Confession of serious sins

**Note:** Sam will not list the specific questions, as they are meant to be asked in the interview setting by authorized leaders.

---

## Issuing Recommends in LCR

### After Both Interviews Complete
**Path:** LCR → Temple → Issue Recommend

1. Search for member
2. Verify both interviews completed
3. Issue recommend
4. System generates recommend number
5. Member takes recommend to temple

### What Gets Recorded
- Issue date
- Expiration date (2 years from issue)
- Who conducted interviews

## Special Situations

### New Members
- Must wait 1 year after baptism for endowment
- Can receive limited-use recommend earlier
- Bishop determines readiness

### Youth Recommends
- Limited-use recommend at age 12
- Bishop interviews (no stake interview needed)
- Renew every 2 years

### Recommend Cancellation
If worthiness issues arise:
- Bishop can cancel recommend
- Prevents temple entry
- Must be resolved before new recommend

### Lost Recommend
- Member requests replacement
- No new interview needed if current period
- Verify identity
- Issue replacement in LCR

### Member Moving
- Recommend transfers with membership record
- Remains valid until expiration
- No need to re-interview just for move

---

## Proactive Management

### Monthly Review Process
1. Pull expiring recommends report (next 60-90 days)
2. Have executive secretary contact members
3. Schedule renewal interviews
4. Follow up on no-shows

### Before Temple Events
If stake temple night or youth baptism trip:
- Check recommend status of participants
- Reach out to those with expired/expiring recommends
- Allow time for interviews

### Tracking Recommend Participation
- Some wards track % of members with recommends
- Can be a goal for ward improvement
- Focus on encouragement, not pressure

## Tips

1. **Be proactive** — Don't wait for members to request renewal
2. **Coordinate with stake** — Know stake presidency availability
3. **Plan ahead** — Especially before temple trips
4. **Maintain confidentiality** — Recommend status is private
5. **Keep interviews timely** — Don't let members' recommends lapse
