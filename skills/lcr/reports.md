# LCR Reports

## Overview

LCR provides various reports for ward administration, tracking, and stake reporting requirements.

## Access

**Path:** LCR → Reports

**Who can access:**
- Ward Clerk — Full access
- Bishopric — Most reports
- Organization presidents — Limited reports for their organization

---

## Quarterly Report

### Purpose
The quarterly report tracks key ward metrics for stake and Church records.

### Deadline
- Due by the end of each quarter
- Stake may set earlier deadlines
- Don't wait until the last day

### What It Includes
- Membership statistics
- Sacrament meeting attendance
- Ministering interview completion
- Other key metrics

### Completing the Quarterly Report
**Path:** LCR → Reports → Quarterly Report

1. Open Quarterly Report section
2. Review pre-populated data
3. Fill in any missing information
4. Verify accuracy
5. Submit

### Tips
- Review data throughout the quarter
- Don't scramble at deadline
- Coordinate with EQ/RS on ministering numbers
- Ask stake clerk if unsure about any field

## Action Items

### What Are Action Items?
Alerts for things needing attention:
- Members turning 8 (baptism)
- Youth ordination ages
- Temple recommend expirations
- Move-in requests pending
- Records needing attention

### Viewing Action Items
**Path:** LCR → Reports → Action Items

Or: Dashboard may show action item count

### Common Action Items
| Item | Action Needed |
|------|---------------|
| Child turning 8 | Schedule baptism interview |
| Young man age 11→12 | Prepare for deacon ordination |
| Young man age 13→14 | Prepare for teacher ordination |
| Young man age 15→16 | Prepare for priest ordination |
| Young man age 17→18 | Prepare for Melchizedek Priesthood |
| Temple recommend expiring | Schedule renewal interview |
| Move-in pending | Accept or investigate |

### Staying on Top of Action Items
- Review weekly (at least)
- Delegate to appropriate people
- Don't let items pile up

## Membership Reports

### New Members
**Path:** LCR → Reports → New Members

Shows:
- Recently baptized converts
- Recent move-ins
- Useful for tracking fellowshipping

### Members Moved Out
**Path:** LCR → Reports → Members Moved

Shows records recently transferred out.

### Prospective Elders
**Path:** LCR → Reports → Prospective Elders

Adult men (18+) who haven't received Melchizedek Priesthood:
- May need reactivation
- May need preparation for ordination
- Coordinate with EQ

### Children of Record
**Path:** LCR → Reports → Children

Children blessed but not yet baptized:
- Track those approaching age 8
- Ensure baptism preparation

---

## Temple Reports

### Temple Recommend Status
**Path:** LCR → Temple → Recommend Status

Shows:
- Members with current recommends
- Expiring recommends (next 30/60/90 days)
- Expired recommends
- Members without recommends

### Expiring Recommends
Review monthly to:
- Contact members proactively
- Schedule renewal interviews
- Especially before stake temple nights

---

## Attendance Reports

### Sacrament Meeting
**Path:** LCR → Reports → Attendance

Track week-by-week attendance:
- Total attendance
- Trends over time
- Useful for quarterly report

### Class Attendance
Individual organizations can track class attendance if desired.

---

## Calling Reports

### Current Callings
**Path:** LCR → Reports → Callings

Full list of all current callings — useful for:
- Ward council planning
- Stake reports
- New bishopric orientation

### Vacancies
**Path:** LCR → Reports → Vacancies

All unfilled positions — prioritize filling critical roles.

### Calling History
**Path:** LCR → Membership → [Member] → Calling History

See a specific member's past service.

---

## Finance Reports

**(Clerk/Bishopric Access Only)**

### Donation Summary
**Path:** LCR → Finance → Reports

- Member donation summaries
- Category totals
- Budget status

### Tithing Settlement
**Path:** LCR → Finance → Tithing Settlement

Used during annual tithing settlement (Nov-Dec):
- Member declaration status
- Donation totals for the year
