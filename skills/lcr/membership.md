# LCR Membership Records

## Overview

Membership records track every member's information, ordinances, and status. Ward clerks and bishopric members manage these records.

## Accessing Membership Records

**Path:** LCR → Membership (left sidebar)

### Member Record View
Each member record includes:
- **Basic info:** Name, birth date, gender, contact info
- **Address:** Current residence
- **Ordinances:** Baptism, confirmation, priesthood, endowment, sealing dates
- **Callings:** Current and past callings
- **Membership status:** Active, less-active, moved, etc.

---

## Move-Ins

When a member's record is transferred to your ward:

### Viewing Pending Move-Ins
**Path:** LCR → Membership → Move-In Requests

1. Click **Move-In Requests** in the Membership section
2. Review pending records
3. For each record:
   - Verify the person actually moved to your ward boundaries
   - Check contact information
   - Accept or reject the transfer

### Accepting a Move-In
1. Click on the member's name
2. Review the record details
3. Click **Accept**
4. The record is now in your ward

### What to Do After Accepting
1. Notify bishopric of new member
2. Ensure EQ/RS president is informed
3. Assign ministering brothers/sisters
4. Welcome visit from bishopric or missionaries

---

## Move-Outs

When a member leaves your ward:

### Processing a Move-Out
**Path:** LCR → Membership → [Find Member] → Move

1. Search for the member
2. Click their name to open record
3. Click **Move** (or "Request Move")
4. Enter new address
5. Select new unit (ward/branch) if known
6. Submit the request

### If New Address Unknown
- Enter partial address or "Address Unknown"
- Record goes to a holding area
- New ward can claim it when they locate the member

### Important Notes
- Records should be moved promptly (don't hold onto records of people who've moved)
- Moving records helps ministering in the new ward
- If member requests, you can delay briefly but not indefinitely

---

## Updating Member Information

### What You Can Update
- Address
- Phone numbers
- Email addresses
- Preferred name
- Household composition

### Path to Update
1. Search for member
2. Click name to open record
3. Click **Edit** (or pencil icon)
4. Make changes
5. Save

### What You Can't Update Directly
Some fields require special processes:
- **Name changes** — legal name changes require documentation
- **Birth dates** — require verification
- **Ordinance dates** — contact stake clerk for corrections
- **Gender** — requires specific approval process

---

## Ordinance Records

### Viewing Ordinance Status
Each member record shows:
- Baptism date and location
- Confirmation date
- Priesthood ordination dates (for males)
- Endowment date
- Sealing date

### Recording New Ordinances
**Path:** LCR → Membership → [Member] → Ordinances

After an ordinance is performed:
1. Open member record
2. Go to Ordinances section
3. Enter ordinance details
4. Save

**Note:** Most ordinances should be recorded within a few days of being performed.

---

## Household Records

### Viewing Household
- Click on any household member
- See "Household" section showing all members
- Shows relationships and individual records

### Updating Household
- Add new baby: Create new record linked to household
- Remove member: They move out or become own household
- Marriage: Link records together

---

## Special Situations

### Records of Deceased Members
- Records remain in ward briefly
- Should be moved to "Deceased" status
- Handled by ward clerk

### Records of Children Turning 18
- May need address update if moving for college/mission
- Update contact information

### Part-Member Families
- Non-member spouse/children show differently
- May have limited information

### Address Confidential Requests
- Some members request address confidentiality
- Their address won't display to other members
- Leaders can still see for official purposes

---

## Reports Related to Membership

**Path:** LCR → Reports

- **New Members:** Recently baptized or moved in
- **Members Moved Out:** Recently transferred records
- **Prospective Elders:** Adult males without Melchizedek Priesthood
- **Children Approaching Baptism:** Turning 8 soon
- **Youth Approaching Mission Age:** For missionary preparation
