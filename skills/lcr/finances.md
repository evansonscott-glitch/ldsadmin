# LCR Finances

## Overview

LCR's finance section handles donation recording, budget tracking, and financial reporting. Access is restricted to ward clerks and bishopric.

## Access

**Path:** LCR → Finance

**Who can access:**
- Ward Clerk — Full access
- Bishop — Full access
- Bishopric Counselors — View access
- **Others:** No access (confidential)

---

## Recording Donations

### Weekly Donation Processing
**Path:** LCR → Finance → Donations → Record

**Process:**
1. Collect donation envelopes after sacrament meeting
2. Count with two people present (dual control)
3. Record each donation in LCR
4. Generate deposit slip
5. Make bank deposit

### Donation Categories
- **Tithing** — 10% of income
- **Fast Offerings** — Monthly fast donations
- **Missionary Fund** — General or specific missionary
- **Humanitarian Aid** — Disaster relief, general welfare
- **Temple Construction** — If applicable
- **Other** — Ward/stake specific funds

### Recording Steps
1. Click **Record Donations**
2. Enter donation date
3. For each donor:
   - Search member name
   - Enter amount(s) by category
   - Verify total matches envelope
4. Save batch
5. Print deposit slip

### Important Rules
- **Dual control:** Always count with two people
- **Same-day processing:** Don't hold donations overnight
- **Accuracy:** Double-check amounts
- **Confidentiality:** Never discuss individual donations

## Budget Management

### Ward Budget
**Path:** LCR → Finance → Budget

The ward receives a budget allocation from the stake based on:
- Ward membership size
- Activity level
- Historical spending

### Viewing Budget
- Total allocation
- Spent to date
- Remaining balance
- By category breakdown

### Budget Categories
- **General Ward** — Misc ward expenses
- **Activities** — Ward activities, youth activities
- **Supplies** — Curriculum, cleaning supplies
- **Equipment** — Larger purchases (stake approval may be needed)

### Spending Within Budget
1. Organization leader identifies need
2. Requests approval from bishopric
3. Makes purchase
4. Submits receipt to clerk
5. Clerk records expense in LCR

---

## Reimbursements

### Requesting Reimbursement
Members who spend personal funds for approved ward expenses:
1. Submit receipt to ward clerk
2. Provide description of expense
3. Wait for check (usually 1-2 weeks)

### Processing Reimbursements
**Path:** LCR → Finance → Expenses

1. Receive receipt and request
2. Verify expense was approved
3. Enter in LCR:
   - Payee name
   - Amount
   - Category
   - Description
4. Generate check (or electronic payment)
5. Obtain signatures as required
6. Deliver to member

### Receipts Required
- Always require receipts
- Original receipts preferred
- Digital copies acceptable

## Tithing Settlement

### Annual Process (November-December)
**Path:** LCR → Finance → Tithing Settlement

Purpose: Members meet with bishop to declare tithing status for the year.

### Before Tithing Settlement
1. Generate donation summaries for all members
2. Print individual reports
3. Schedule appointments (exec secretary helps)

### During Settlement
1. Bishop meets with individual/family
2. Reviews donation summary
3. Member declares status:
   - **Full tithe payer**
   - **Partial tithe payer**
   - **Non-tithe payer**
4. Clerk records declaration in LCR

### Deadline
- Complete by December 15 (stake may vary)
- Allows time for corrections before year-end

### Reports
- Track completion rate
- Follow up with members not scheduled

## Annual Audit

### Purpose
Stake auditors verify proper financial handling:
- Donation procedures followed
- Dual control maintained
- Receipts on file
- Budget properly managed

### Preparing for Audit
1. Keep all receipts organized
2. Maintain donation records
3. Document all expenses
4. Follow proper procedures year-round

### Common Audit Findings
- Missing receipts
- Donations not counted with dual control
- Budget overspending without approval
- Late deposit of donations

---

## Best Practices

### Donation Handling
- Process donations same day as received
- Always use dual control
- Lock donations securely until processed
- Never take donations home

### Budget
- Track spending throughout the year
- Don't overspend allocation
- Get approval for large expenses
- Plan major expenses in advance

### Confidentiality
- Never discuss individual donations
- Donation reports are confidential
- Only bishop and clerk see amounts
- Don't leave donation screens visible

### Documentation
- Keep receipts for 7 years (or per local policy)
- Document unusual transactions
- Maintain audit trail

## Troubleshooting

**Donation doesn't match envelope:**
- Re-count carefully
- Contact donor if discrepancy is significant

**Need to correct a donation:**
- Corrections can be made in LCR
- Document reason for correction
- May need bishopric awareness

**Budget overspent:**
- Contact stake for guidance
- May need to reduce spending elsewhere
- Document circumstances
