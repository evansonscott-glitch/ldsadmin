# LCR Common Tasks — Step-by-Step

Quick reference for the most frequent LCR tasks.

---

## Accept a Move-In

**When:** New member's record transferred to your ward

1. Log into LCR
2. Click **Membership** (left sidebar)
3. Click **Move-In Requests**
4. Find the pending record
5. Review details (name, previous ward, address)
6. Click the record → **Accept**
7. Notify bishopric of new member
8. Ensure ministering assignment is made

---

## Move a Member Out

**When:** Member has moved to another ward's boundaries

1. Log into LCR
2. Click **Membership**
3. Search for the member's name
4. Click on their record
5. Click **Move** (or "Request Move Out")
6. Enter new address (as much as known)
7. Select new ward if known (or leave for system to route)
8. Submit

---

## Update Member Contact Info

**When:** Phone number, email, or address changes

1. Log into LCR
2. Click **Membership**
3. Search for member
4. Click on their record
5. Click **Edit** (pencil icon)
6. Update phone, email, or address
7. Save

---

## Record a New Calling

**When:** After someone is sustained in sacrament meeting

1. Log into LCR
2. Click **Organizations** (left sidebar)
3. Select the organization (e.g., Primary, Elders Quorum)
4. Find the position or click **Add Calling**
5. Search for and select the member
6. Enter sustaining date
7. Mark as **Sustained**
8. After setting apart, return and mark **Set Apart**
9. Save

---

## Release Someone from a Calling

**When:** After they're released in sacrament meeting

1. Log into LCR
2. Click **Organizations**
3. Find the person's current calling
4. Click on the calling
5. Click **Release**
6. Enter release date
7. Save

---

## Update Ministering Assignments

**When:** Changing who ministers to whom

### Add a Companionship
1. Click **Ministering** (left sidebar)
2. Click **Companionships**
3. Click **Add Companionship**
4. Select the ministering brothers/sisters
5. Assign households to them
6. Save

### Reassign a Household
1. Click **Ministering** → **Companionships**
2. Find the current companionship
3. Edit → Remove the household
4. Find the new companionship
5. Edit → Add the household
6. Save

---

## Record a Ministering Interview

**When:** After EQ/RS presidency member conducts interview

1. Click **Ministering**
2. Click **Interviews**
3. Click **Record Interview**
4. Select the companionship
5. Enter interview date
6. Select who conducted
7. Add brief notes if needed
8. Save

---

## Check Temple Recommend Expirations

**When:** Monthly review, before temple trips

1. Click **Temple** (left sidebar)
2. Click **Expiring Recommends**
3. Filter by timeframe (30, 60, or 90 days)
4. Review the list
5. Contact members to schedule renewals

---

## Issue a Temple Recommend

**When:** After both bishopric and stake interviews complete

1. Click **Temple**
2. Click **Issue Recommend**
3. Search for member
4. Verify interviews are complete
5. Issue recommend
6. Give recommend to member (or confirm they received it)

---

## View/Complete Quarterly Report

**When:** End of each quarter

1. Click **Reports** (left sidebar)
2. Click **Quarterly Report**
3. Review pre-filled data
4. Fill in any blanks (attendance, ministering %)
5. Verify accuracy
6. Click **Submit**

---

## Check Action Items

**When:** Weekly (or more often)

1. Look at dashboard for action item count
2. Or click **Reports** → **Action Items**
3. Review each item:
   - Children turning 8 → coordinate baptism
   - Youth ordination ages → coordinate with bishopric
   - Expiring recommends → schedule interviews
   - Pending move-ins → accept or investigate
4. Take action or delegate
5. Check off completed items

---

## Record Donations (Weekly)

**When:** After sacrament meeting, with two people present

1. Collect donation envelopes
2. Count donations with dual control
3. Log into LCR
4. Click **Finance** → **Donations** → **Record**
5. Enter donation date
6. For each envelope:
   - Search member name
   - Enter amount(s) by category
   - Verify total
7. Save batch
8. Print deposit slip
9. Make bank deposit

---

## Process a Reimbursement

**When:** Member submits receipt for approved ward expense

1. Receive receipt and description
2. Verify expense was pre-approved
3. Click **Finance** → **Expenses**
4. Click **Add Expense**
5. Enter payee, amount, category, description
6. Attach receipt image if possible
7. Generate check
8. Get required signatures
9. Deliver to member

---

## Find Ward Vacancies

**When:** Bishopric planning meeting

1. Click **Reports**
2. Click **Vacancies**
3. Review unfilled positions
4. Prioritize critical callings
5. Begin prayerful consideration

---

## Print Ward Directory / Calling List

**When:** Ward council, new bishopric, stake requests

1. Click **Reports**
2. Select report type:
   - **Callings** — Full calling list
   - **Member Directory** — Contact info (for authorized use)
3. Set any filters
4. Click **Export** or **Print**

---

## Search for a Member

**When:** Need to find someone quickly

1. Use the **Search bar** at top of LCR
2. Type name (partial is fine)
3. Click on result to view record
4. From there: view details, edit, move, see callings, etc.
