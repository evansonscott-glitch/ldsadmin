# LCR Ministering

## Overview

LCR's ministering section helps EQ and RS presidencies manage ministering assignments, track interviews, and coordinate care for members.

## Access

**Path:** LCR → Ministering

**Who can access:**
- EQ Presidency — Elders quorum ministering
- RS Presidency — Relief Society ministering
- Bishopric — Full view of both

---

## Viewing Current Assignments

### Ministering Overview
**Path:** LCR → Ministering → Overview

Shows:
- Total companionships
- Assigned vs unassigned members
- Interview completion rate
- Recent changes

### By Companionship
**Path:** LCR → Ministering → Companionships

Lists all companionships with:
- Ministering brothers/sisters
- Assigned households
- Interview status
- Last contact

### By Household
**Path:** LCR → Ministering → Households

Lists all households showing:
- Who is assigned to minister to them
- Interview status
- Any notes or flags

---

## Making Assignments

### Assign a Companionship
**Path:** LCR → Ministering → Companionships → Add/Edit

1. Click **Add Companionship** or edit existing
2. Select ministering brothers/sisters (usually 2)
3. Assign households to this companionship
4. Save

### Assignment Considerations
- **Personalities:** Match compatible ministering companions
- **Geography:** Minimize travel when possible
- **Life stage:** Consider matching similar circumstances
- **Capacity:** Don't overload any companionship
- **New members:** Pair experienced with newer

### Unassigned Members
**Path:** LCR → Ministering → Unassigned

Shows members without ministering assignments:
- Both those without someone assigned to them
- And those not assigned to minister to anyone

## Recording Interviews

### What to Record
After conducting a ministering interview:
- Interview date
- Who conducted
- Brief notes (optional, keep confidential)
- Needs identified

### Recording an Interview
**Path:** LCR → Ministering → Interviews → Record

1. Select the companionship
2. Enter interview date
3. Select interviewer
4. Add any notes
5. Save

### Interview Tracking
**Path:** LCR → Ministering → Interviews

View:
- Interviews completed this quarter
- Interviews overdue
- Interview history

### Interview Frequency
- Minimum: Quarterly
- More often for companionships with challenging assignments
- Track trends over time

## Reporting to Ward Council

### Preparing a Report
Before ward council, review:
1. **Unassigned members** — who needs coverage?
2. **Identified needs** — who needs extra help?
3. **Interview completion** — are we on track?

### What to Share (Confidentially)
- Families needing extra support
- Members struggling (without unnecessary detail)
- Gaps in coverage
- Successful ministering stories (anonymized if needed)

### What NOT to Share
- Specific confession-level details
- Gossip or speculation
- Anything the member asked to keep private

## Special Situations

### Youth Companions
- Youth (14+) can be assigned as junior companions
- Must be paired with an adult
- Good training opportunity

### Married Couples
- Can minister together as a companionship
- Often assigned to other couples

### Less-Active Members
- May need more persistent, patient ministering
- Assign experienced, patient ministers
- Coordinate with missionaries if appropriate

### New Move-Ins
- Assign ministering quickly
- First impressions matter
- Coordinate welcome from multiple sources

### Members Who Don't Want Ministering
- Respect their wishes
- Keep assignment light-touch
- Be available if they change their mind

---

## Coordination with EQ/RS

### Shared Responsibility
- **EQ President:** Manages ministering brothers
- **RS President:** Manages ministering sisters
- **Coordination:** Both serve the same families

### How to Coordinate
- Regular check-ins between EQ and RS presidents
- Joint discussion in ward council
- Share information about family needs
- Avoid duplication of effort

### Who Handles What?
- **EQ:** Typically handles service projects (moves, repairs)
- **RS:** Typically handles meals, emotional support
- **Both:** Spiritual welfare, general friendship

## Tips for Effective Use

1. **Update regularly** — Changes in LCR help everyone stay current
2. **Keep notes brief** — Don't over-document
3. **Review before meetings** — Come to ward council prepared
4. **Use the reports** — They highlight gaps automatically
5. **Coordinate, don't duplicate** — EQ and RS should talk
