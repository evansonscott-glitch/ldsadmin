# Ward Mission Leader Playbook

## Overview

The ward mission leader coordinates missionary work in the ward. This includes supporting full-time missionaries, encouraging member involvement, and helping retain and activate converts.

**Handbook Reference:** Chapter 23
**Reports to:** Bishop (and coordinates with EQ president)
**Typical Duration:** 2-4 years

## Key Responsibilities

### Missionary Coordination
- Coordinate with full-time missionaries
- Attend coordination meetings
- Share ward mission plan with members
- Track investigators and new members

### Member Missionary Work
- Encourage members to share the gospel
- Help members prepare names for missionaries
- Facilitate member-missionary activities
- Reduce fear of sharing

### Convert Retention
- Ensure new members are fellowshipped
- Coordinate ministering for new converts
- Help new members get callings
- Support temple preparation

### Activation
- Coordinate with EQ/RS on less-active members
- Support missionary efforts with part-member families

## Typical Meetings

| Meeting | Frequency | Duration | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Missionary coordination | Weekly | 30-45 min | Coordinate with full-time missionaries |
| Ward council | Monthly | 60-90 min | Report on missionary work |
| Ward mission coordination | As needed | Varies | Plan activities, events |

## Common Workflows

### Weekly Missionary Coordination
1. Meet with full-time missionaries
2. Review teaching pool
3. Discuss member referrals
4. Plan member involvement in lessons
5. Identify needs and remove obstacles
6. Coordinate with EQ/RS as needed

### Member Referral Process
1. Encourage members to think of friends
2. Make it easy to share names
3. Connect missionaries with members for introductions
4. Follow up on referrals
5. Thank members for participating

### New Convert Retention
1. Know every new convert
2. Ensure ministering is assigned immediately
3. Meet personally within first week
4. Help them get a calling
5. Prepare for temple (1 year)
6. Check in regularly for first year

### Part-Member Family Outreach
1. Identify part-member families
2. Coordinate with missionaries
3. Involve member spouse appropriately
4. Be patient and consistent
5. Focus on friendship first

## Tips for Success

### Full-Time Missionaries
- Build a real relationship
- Help them succeed (they're often young and learning)
- Find them meals and teaching appointments
- Give honest feedback

### Member Missionary Work
- Make it easy and natural
- Share success stories
- Don't guilt people — inspire them
- Focus on relationships, not cold contacts

### Retention
- The first year is critical
- New members need friends, responsibilities, and nurturing
- Callings help people stay active
- Temple preparation gives direction

### Coordination
- You're a coordinator, not a lone ranger
- Work with EQ, RS, and missionaries together
- Report to ward council — keep it visible
- Celebrate baptisms and progress

## Common Challenges

### "We don't have anyone to teach"
- Focus on member relationships
- Help members identify friends naturally
- Part-member families are opportunities
- Less-active members may be ready to return

### Low Member Involvement
- Make it easy (specific asks, not vague)
- Invite to specific lessons
- Share positive experiences
- Don't overwhelm — start small

### New Converts Going Inactive
- Act fast — reach out immediately
- Multiple touchpoints (ministering, calling, activities)
- Address concerns directly
- Involve their friends

## Yearly Focus

| Period | Focus |
|--------|-------|
| New Year | Set ward mission goals |
| Spring | Easter outreach opportunities |
| Summer | Service projects with community |
| Fall | Community events, harvest |
| December | Christmas outreach |

## Red Flags to Watch

- Missionaries meeting alone (no member involvement)
- New converts not fellowshipped
- No member referrals
- Missionaries frustrated or unsupported
- Less-active members ignored

## Sam's Role

Sam can help ward mission leaders with:
- Tracking investigators and new converts
- Coordinating missionary meals
- Planning member-missionary activities
- Following up on new member retention
- Communication with ward council
- Remembering member referrals and their status

Sam should NOT:
- Make decisions about investigators without missionary input
- Contact investigators directly
- Replace personal relationship with missionaries and converts
