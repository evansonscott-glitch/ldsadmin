# Bishop Playbook

## Overview

The bishop is the presiding high priest in the ward, holding priesthood keys for the ward. He is responsible for the temporal and spiritual welfare of all ward members.

**Handbook Reference:** Chapter 7
**Reports to:** Stake President
**Typical Duration:** ~5 years

## Key Responsibilities

### Presiding
- Preside at all ward meetings
- Hold priesthood keys for the ward
- Oversee all ward organizations
- Conduct temple recommend interviews
- Judge worthiness for ordinances

### Welfare & Temporal Needs
- Administer fast offerings
- Coordinate temporal assistance
- Oversee welfare resources
- Help members become self-reliant

### Youth & Young Single Adults
- Conduct youth interviews (annually, and for ordinances)
- Oversee Aaronic Priesthood (priests quorum president)
- Support young single adults

### Counseling & Confession
- Receive confessions
- Provide counsel and support
- Administer Church discipline when needed

### Ordinances
- Oversee all ward ordinances
- Authorize baptisms, blessings, etc.
- Approve priesthood ordinations

## Typical Weekly Meetings

| Meeting | Frequency | Duration | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Bishopric meeting | Weekly | 60-90 min | Plan, coordinate, review needs |
| PEC | Weekly/Biweekly | 45-60 min | Welfare, ministering, confidential matters |
| Ward council | Monthly | 60-90 min | Coordinate organizations, discuss ward needs |
| Sacrament meeting | Weekly | 60 min | Preside and conduct worship |
| Youth interviews | Ongoing | 15-30 min each | Individual meetings with youth |

## Common Workflows

### Temple Recommend Interviews
1. Schedule interview (exec secretary manages)
2. Review membership record in LCR
3. Conduct interview using recommend questions
4. If approved, sign recommend in LCR
5. Member completes interview with stake presidency member

### Welfare Assistance
1. Receive request (direct or through ministering)
2. Review situation with RS president
3. Meet with individual/family
4. Create self-reliance plan
5. Provide temporary assistance as needed
6. Follow up on progress

### Extending Callings
1. Pray about whom to call
2. Counsel with bishopric
3. Clear calling with stake (if required)
4. Interview for worthiness (if required)
5. Extend calling
6. Present for sustaining
7. Set apart
8. Ensure training

### Youth Interviews
- **Annual interviews:** Review gospel living, goals, challenges
- **Ordinance interviews:** Baptism (8), priesthood advancement, temple recommends
- **As-needed:** When youth or parents request

## Tips for Success

### Time Management
- Block calendar for family time — protect it
- Delegate everything delegable
- Use executive secretary well
- Counsel with counselors; don't do everything yourself

### Counselors
- Give them real responsibility
- Assign specific oversight areas
- Trust their judgment
- Meet together, not just individually

### Welfare
- RS president is your partner — involve her early
- Focus on self-reliance, not just assistance
- Fast offerings are sacred; be thoughtful but not stingy
- Document decisions

### Youth
- Know every youth by name
- Be approachable
- Interviews should be comfortable, not intimidating
- Partner with parents

### Avoiding Burnout
- This calling is demanding — pace yourself
- Rely on the Lord; you can't do it alone
- Your family still needs you
- It's okay to say "not tonight"

## Red Flags to Watch

- Counselors feeling underutilized
- RS president not in welfare discussions
- Youth avoiding interviews
- Ward council becoming a reporting meeting instead of a council
- Your family feeling neglected

## Sam's Role

Sam can help bishops with:
- Scheduling and calendar management
- Interview tracking
- Meeting agendas and follow-ups
- Remembering member situations
- Drafting communications
- Tracking welfare cases (appropriately)

Sam should NOT:
- Make worthiness judgments
- Access confidential confession information
- Replace personal ministering
