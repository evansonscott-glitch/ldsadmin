# Executive Secretary Playbook

## Overview

The executive secretary is the bishopric's right hand for scheduling, communication, and coordination. This calling keeps the bishopric organized and ensures nothing falls through the cracks.

**Handbook Reference:** Chapter 33
**Reports to:** Bishop
**Typical Duration:** Variable (often 2-4 years)

## Key Responsibilities

### Scheduling
- Schedule appointments with the bishop
- Manage bishopric calendars
- Coordinate youth interviews
- Schedule temple recommend interviews

### Communication
- Relay messages to/from bishopric
- Coordinate meeting invitations
- Follow up on assignments
- Ward communications as directed

### Meeting Support
- Prepare agendas
- Attend bishopric meeting
- Track action items
- Ensure follow-up happens

### Sacrament Meeting Support
- Coordinate speaking assignments
- Ensure prayers and music are arranged
- Prepare programs
- Handle last-minute changes

## Typical Meetings

| Meeting | Frequency | Duration | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Bishopric meeting | Weekly | 60-90 min | Support, track action items |
| Coordination with bishop | As needed | Brief | Scheduling, updates |

## Common Workflows

### Scheduling Bishop Interviews
1. Member requests appointment
2. Check bishop's availability
3. Schedule in calendar
4. Confirm with member (time, location)
5. Remind member day before
6. Remind bishop of schedule

### Temple Recommend Appointments
1. Member requests recommend interview
2. Schedule bishopric interview first
3. After approval, schedule stake presidency interview
4. Coordinate timing so member can complete both efficiently

### Youth Interview Scheduling
1. Coordinate with YM/YW leaders on interview needs
2. Block time in bishop's calendar
3. Send schedule to youth and parents
4. Send reminders
5. Track completion

### Sacrament Meeting Coordination
1. Get speaker assignments from bishopric
2. Contact speakers and confirm topics
3. Arrange prayers (opening, closing)
4. Coordinate with music director
5. Prepare program or ensure it's prepared
6. Handle day-of changes gracefully

### Action Item Tracking
1. Record assignments from bishopric meeting
2. Note who is responsible and deadline
3. Follow up before next meeting
4. Report status at next meeting
5. Escalate stuck items

## Tips for Success

### Be the Gatekeeper (Wisely)
- Protect bishop's time — screen requests appropriately
- But don't be a barrier — urgent needs get through
- Know what the bishop wants to handle vs. delegate

### Communication
- Respond promptly to requests
- Confirm everything in writing
- Follow up before people forget
- Be pleasant — you're often the first contact

### Organization
- Maintain clean calendars
- Track everything in one system
- Prepare ahead for recurring events
- Anticipate needs

### Sacrament Meeting
- Build a rotation of speakers several weeks out
- Have backup plans (last-minute cancellations happen)
- Brief speakers on time limits
- Keep the program simple and accurate

### Working with Bishopric
- Learn their preferences
- Anticipate their needs
- Don't surprise them
- Handle details so they can focus on people

## Calendar Management Tips

### Bishop's Calendar
- Block family time — protect it
- Allow buffer between interviews
- Cluster interviews when possible
- Note what each appointment is for

### Ward Calendar
- Keep it current
- Coordinate with all organizations
- Avoid conflicts
- Communicate changes quickly

## Red Flags to Watch

- Appointments falling through cracks
- Speakers confirmed last-minute
- Action items not being tracked
- Bishop's calendar overloaded
- Poor communication with ward members

## Sam's Role

Sam can help executive secretaries with:
- Scheduling and calendar management
- Sending reminders to bishop and members
- Tracking action items from meetings
- Coordinating speaker assignments
- Drafting ward communications
- Preparing agendas

Sam should NOT:
- Make scheduling decisions without exec secretary's input
- Access confidential interview details
- Communicate on behalf of bishop without approval
