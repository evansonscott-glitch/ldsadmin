# Elders Quorum President Playbook

## Overview

The elders quorum president leads the Melchizedek Priesthood holders in the ward. He focuses on strengthening quorum members, coordinating ministering, and leading service efforts.

**Handbook Reference:** Chapter 8
**Reports to:** Bishop (and coordinates with stake high councilor)
**Typical Duration:** 3-5 years

## Key Responsibilities

### Quorum Leadership
- Preside over the elders quorum
- Lead quorum meetings
- Conduct quorum presidency meetings
- Strengthen less-active and prospective elders

### Ministering (with RS President)
- Organize ministering companionships
- Conduct ministering interviews
- Report to ward council on member needs
- Coordinate with RS on family needs

### Service & Welfare
- Coordinate service projects
- Respond to member needs (moves, repairs, etc.)
- Support bishops' welfare efforts
- Help members become self-reliant

### Missionary Support
- Support full-time missionaries
- Encourage member missionary work
- Help with convert retention

## Typical Meetings

| Meeting | Frequency | Duration | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Quorum presidency meeting | Weekly | 30-45 min | Plan, assign, coordinate |
| Quorum meeting | 2nd & 4th Sundays | 50 min | Instruction, business, brotherhood |
| Ward council | Monthly | 60-90 min | Coordinate with other organizations |
| PEC | Weekly/Biweekly | 45-60 min | Welfare and ministering |
| Ministering interviews | Quarterly+ | 15-20 min each | Check on assigned families |

## Common Workflows

### Ministering Assignments
1. Review member needs with RS president
2. Prayerfully assign companionships
3. Consider personalities, needs, and geography
4. Update assignments in LCR
5. Communicate new assignments
6. Conduct quarterly interviews

### Ministering Interviews
1. Schedule interviews (presidency divides assignments)
2. Ask about assigned individuals/families
3. Discuss specific needs discovered
4. Offer support and resources
5. Report significant needs to bishop (confidentially)

### Service Requests
1. Receive request (moves, yard work, home repairs)
2. Assess scope and timing
3. Recruit volunteers (text/call, use quorum group chat)
4. Coordinate logistics (tools, timing)
5. Follow up to ensure completion

### Activating Less-Active Members
1. Identify less-active members (ministering reports, records)
2. Assign ministering companionships who can connect
3. Personal outreach from presidency
4. Find meaningful ways to involve them
5. Be patient — this takes time

## Tips for Success

### Presidency Teamwork
- Meet weekly (brief is fine)
- Divide responsibilities clearly
- Counselors should have real ownership
- Secretary tracks action items

### Ministering
- It's about relationships, not checkboxes
- Assign based on connection potential
- Follow up on concerning situations
- Coordinate closely with RS president

### Quorum Meetings
- Make them worth attending
- Focus on application, not lecture
- Include brotherhood time
- Address real challenges men face

### Service
- Respond quickly to needs
- Don't burn out the same 10 people
- Make it easy to help (clear asks, specific times)
- Thank volunteers

### Working with the Bishop
- Keep him informed but don't overload
- Bring solutions, not just problems
- Support his vision for the ward
- Handle what you can; escalate what you can't

## Red Flags to Watch

- Ministering interviews not happening
- Same people doing all the service
- Quorum meetings poorly attended
- Less-active members not being contacted
- RS president not included in welfare discussions

## Sam's Role

Sam can help EQ presidents with:
- Tracking ministering assignments and interviews
- Scheduling service projects
- Quorum meeting agendas
- Following up on action items
- Remembering member needs and situations
- Coordinating with RS president

Sam should NOT:
- Make assignments without the president's input
- Access confidential welfare information without permission
- Replace personal outreach
