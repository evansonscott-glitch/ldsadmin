# Sunday School President Playbook

## Overview

The Sunday School president oversees gospel instruction in the ward. The focus is on helping teachers teach effectively and helping members learn deeply.

**Handbook Reference:** Chapter 13
**Reports to:** Bishop
**Typical Duration:** 3-5 years

## Key Responsibilities

### Gospel Teaching Quality
- Support and train teachers
- Ensure doctrinally sound instruction
- Help teachers improve
- Model good teaching

### Teacher Support
- Call and orient new teachers
- Provide resources
- Observe classes and give feedback
- Teacher council meetings

### Class Organization
- Determine class structure (by age, topic, etc.)
- Manage class assignments
- Handle visitor and new member integration

## Typical Meetings

| Meeting | Frequency | Duration | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Presidency meeting | Weekly/Biweekly | 20-30 min | Coordinate, discuss teachers |
| Sunday School | 1st & 3rd Sundays | 50 min | Gospel instruction |
| Teacher council | Quarterly | 30-45 min | Training, sharing, improvement |
| Ward council | Monthly | 60-90 min | Coordinate with other organizations |

## Common Workflows

### Teacher Council Meeting
1. Schedule quarterly (or as needed)
2. Invite all ward teachers (not just Sunday School)
3. Focus on one teaching principle
4. Share experiences and ideas
5. Practice techniques
6. Keep it practical and encouraging

### Supporting a Struggling Teacher
1. Observe class to understand the challenge
2. Meet privately (encourage, don't criticize)
3. Offer specific suggestions
4. Provide resources
5. Follow up with support
6. If needed, counsel with bishopric

### New Teacher Orientation
1. Meet with new teacher
2. Explain curriculum and resources
3. Share teaching principles
4. Connect with experienced teachers
5. Visit their first few classes
6. Provide feedback and encouragement

### Class Structure Decisions
1. Assess class sizes and needs
2. Counsel with bishopric
3. Consider combining or splitting classes
4. Ensure teachers are available
5. Communicate changes

## Tips for Success

### Focus on Teachers
- This calling is about helping teachers succeed
- Every ward teacher benefits from support
- Quality teaching = changed lives

### Teaching Principles
- Focus on doctrine, not just stories
- Invite the Spirit
- Discussion > lecture
- Application matters — "What will you do?"
- Use scriptures, not just lesson manual

### Teacher Council
- This is your most important tool
- Keep it practical and uplifting
- Teachers learn from each other
- Don't lecture — model good teaching

### Light Touch
- Don't micromanage teachers
- Offer support, not supervision
- Encourage experimentation
- Celebrate successes

### Class Attendance
- Notice who's not coming
- Classes should be worth attending
- Variety and quality matter

## Resources for Teachers

- **Come, Follow Me:** Primary curriculum for home and church
- **Teaching in the Savior's Way:** Core teaching resource
- **Gospel Library app:** Scriptures, manuals, videos
- **ChurchofJesusChrist.org:** Additional resources

## Red Flags to Watch

- Teachers struggling without support
- Classes poorly attended
- Teacher council not happening
- Same people always teaching (or no one willing)
- Doctrine being taught incorrectly

## Sam's Role

Sam can help Sunday School presidents with:
- Tracking teacher assignments
- Planning teacher council agendas
- Sharing teaching resources
- Meeting coordination
- Following up with teachers

Sam should NOT:
- Evaluate doctrine being taught
- Make calling recommendations alone
- Replace personal interaction with teachers
