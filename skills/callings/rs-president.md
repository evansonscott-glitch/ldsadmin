# Relief Society President Playbook

## Overview

The Relief Society president leads the women of the ward (18+). She focuses on strengthening sisters, coordinating ministering, and responding to temporal and spiritual needs.

**Handbook Reference:** Chapter 9
**Reports to:** Bishop
**Typical Duration:** 3-5 years

## Key Responsibilities

### Sisterhood & Spiritual Strength
- Preside over Relief Society
- Lead Sunday meetings
- Plan activities that build unity
- Support sisters in their covenants

### Ministering (with EQ President)
- Organize ministering companionships for sisters
- Conduct ministering interviews
- Report to ward council on member needs
- Coordinate with EQ on family needs

### Welfare & Compassionate Service
- Key partner with bishop on welfare
- Coordinate meals, service, support
- Visit and assess needs
- Help sisters become self-reliant

### Support for Specific Groups
- Young single adults
- Single mothers
- Elderly sisters
- Sisters with disabilities
- Sisters new to the ward

## Typical Meetings

| Meeting | Frequency | Duration | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Presidency meeting | Weekly | 30-45 min | Plan, coordinate, discuss sisters |
| RS meeting | 2nd & 4th Sundays | 50 min | Instruction and sisterhood |
| Ward council | Monthly | 60-90 min | Coordinate with other organizations |
| PEC | Weekly/Biweekly | 45-60 min | Welfare and ministering |
| Ministering interviews | Quarterly+ | 15-20 min each | Check on assigned sisters/families |

## Common Workflows

### Ministering Assignments
1. Review sister needs with EQ president
2. Prayerfully assign companionships
3. Consider personalities, life stages, geography
4. Update assignments in LCR
5. Communicate new assignments
6. Conduct quarterly interviews

### Welfare Assessment
1. Learn of a need (ministering, bishop, direct request)
2. Visit the sister/family
3. Assess immediate and long-term needs
4. Counsel with bishop
5. Coordinate assistance (meals, service, resources)
6. Follow up and support self-reliance

### Meal Coordination
1. Receive request (new baby, illness, death, etc.)
2. Assess duration and dietary needs
3. Create sign-up (MealTrain, Google Sheet, etc.)
4. Communicate to ward/quorum
5. Ensure coverage
6. Thank participants

### Supporting New Members & Move-Ins
1. Welcome personally (visit or call)
2. Assign ministering sisters
3. Introduce to other sisters
4. Include in activities
5. Help them find their place

## Tips for Success

### Presidency Teamwork
- Meet weekly (even briefly)
- Divide oversight areas (activities, ministering, specific groups)
- Counselors should lead, not just help
- Secretary is invaluable — use them

### Ministering
- Relationships over reports
- Pair thoughtfully (life stage, interests, personalities)
- Follow up on concerning situations
- Coordinate with EQ — you serve the same families

### Welfare Partnership with Bishop
- You are his key partner
- Visit and assess before he makes decisions
- Bring complete pictures, not just requests
- Help create self-reliance plans
- Follow up on families receiving assistance

### Building Sisterhood
- Activities matter — plan meaningful ones
- Not everyone likes the same things
- Include sisters on the margins
- Sunday meetings should inspire, not lecture

### Self-Care
- This calling can be emotionally heavy
- You don't have to carry everything
- Rely on your counselors
- Protect your family time

## Red Flags to Watch

- Ministering interviews not happening
- Sisters falling through the cracks
- Bishop making welfare decisions without your input
- Same people doing all the service
- Presidency burning out

## Sam's Role

Sam can help RS presidents with:
- Tracking ministering assignments and interviews
- Coordinating meals and service
- Meeting agendas and follow-ups
- Remembering sister needs and situations
- Activity planning logistics
- Coordinating with EQ president

Sam should NOT:
- Make assignments without the president's input
- Access confidential welfare information without permission
- Replace personal visits and connection
