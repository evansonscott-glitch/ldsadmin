# Ward Clerk Playbook

## Overview

The ward clerk handles records, finances, and administrative functions. This calling supports the bishopric by managing the administrative machinery of the ward.

**Handbook Reference:** Chapter 33
**Reports to:** Bishop
**Typical Duration:** Variable (often 3-5 years)

## Key Responsibilities

### Membership Records
- Process move-ins and move-outs
- Update member information
- Track ordinances
- Manage records in LCR

### Finances
- Manage tithing and donations
- Process fast offerings
- Prepare financial reports
- Maintain donation confidentiality

### Reports & Compliance
- Quarterly reports
- Annual tithing declaration
- Audit preparation
- Temple recommend tracking

### Meeting Support
- Attend bishopric meeting and PEC
- Take notes and track action items
- Prepare statistical reports

## Typical Meetings

| Meeting | Frequency | Duration | Purpose |
|---------|-----------|----------|---------|
| Bishopric meeting | Weekly | 60-90 min | Support, take notes, report |
| PEC | Weekly/Biweekly | 45-60 min | Support, take notes |
| Tithing settlement | Annually (Nov-Dec) | Varies | Process declarations |

## Common Workflows

### Processing Move-Ins
1. Receive notification in LCR
2. Review record for completeness
3. Accept record into ward
4. Notify bishopric of new member
5. Ensure RS/EQ presidents are informed
6. Verify contact information

### Processing Move-Outs
1. Member notifies of move
2. Create move request in LCR
3. Transfer record to new ward/unit
4. Update any local tracking

### Weekly Donation Processing
1. Collect donation envelopes
2. Count and record donations (with second person present)
3. Enter in LCR
4. Prepare deposit
5. Issue receipts as needed

### Quarterly Report
1. Review membership statistics
2. Verify ordinance records
3. Complete quarterly report in LCR
4. Review with bishop
5. Submit by deadline

### Tithing Settlement
1. Schedule appointments (work with exec secretary)
2. Prepare individual donation summaries
3. Support bishop during interviews
4. Record declarations in LCR
5. Complete by December 15

### Audit Preparation
1. Ensure donation records are complete
2. Reconcile financial records
3. Verify procedures are followed
4. Cooperate with stake audit
5. Address any findings

## LCR Navigation Tips

### Key Sections
- **Membership:** Move-ins, move-outs, record updates
- **Finance:** Donations, budgets, reports
- **Leader Resources:** Calling lists, temple recommends
- **Reports:** Quarterly, annual, statistical

### Common Tasks
- Accept move-in: Member Records → Move-In Requests
- Update info: Individual record → Edit
- Record donation: Finance → Donations → Record
- Run report: Reports → Select type → Generate

## Tips for Success

### Accuracy Matters
- Double-check everything — records affect ordinances and temple access
- When in doubt, verify with the member
- Keep current on LCR changes and training

### Confidentiality
- Donation amounts are sacred and private
- Never discuss contributions with anyone except bishopric
- Financial records should be secured

### Timeliness
- Process move-ins promptly (people need records for recommends)
- Don't let donations pile up
- Meet all reporting deadlines

### Working with Bishopric
- Be proactive — anticipate what they need
- Prepare reports before they ask
- Maintain organized notes from meetings

### Organization
- Create systems that work for you
- Track deadlines
- Keep clean records
- Document unusual situations

## Red Flags to Watch

- Move-in records sitting unprocessed
- Donation processing delays
- Missed reporting deadlines
- Audit findings not addressed
- Confidentiality concerns

## Sam's Role

Sam can help ward clerks with:
- Reminders for deadlines (quarterly reports, tithing settlement)
- Tracking action items from bishopric meetings
- Preparing checklists for recurring tasks
- Navigating LCR procedures
- Organizing tithing settlement scheduling

Sam should NOT:
- Access actual financial records or amounts
- Process donations (requires physical presence and dual control)
- Make judgment calls on records without clerk's direction
