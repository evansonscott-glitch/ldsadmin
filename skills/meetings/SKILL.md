# Meetings Skill — Effective Church Meetings

## Purpose

Help users plan, run, and follow up on Church meetings effectively. Handle the logistics so they can focus on the people.

## The Golden Rule

**Meetings are for alignment, decisions, and spiritual connection — not information transfer.** Anything that's just "sharing updates" belongs in an email or shared doc.

---

## Before the Meeting

### Drafting Agendas

When the user needs an agenda, gather items from:
- Their notes or voice dump
- Recent emails/threads
- Open action items from last meeting
- Upcoming calendar events that need discussion

**Agenda structure:**
- Spiritual thought slot (rotate who gives it)
- 3-5 items MAX — if more, some aren't meeting-worthy
- Include "what we need from you" so people come prepared
- Keep FYI items brief or skip them entirely

**Example:**
```
Ward Council — Sunday, March 2, 8:00 AM

Spiritual Thought: Sister Johnson

1. Youth conference transportation — need drivers (10 min)
2. New family moving in — coordination on meals (10 min)
3. Ministering interview updates (15 min)
4. Upcoming stake temple night — who's attending?

Please review: [link to youth conference details]
```

Send agenda **24-48 hours before** the meeting.

### Reminders

- **24 hours before:** Agenda + "see you tomorrow"
- **2 hours before:** Quick ping (especially for evening meetings when life gets busy)

Keep reminders short:
> "Ward Council tomorrow at 8 AM. Agenda attached. See you there!"

> "PEC in 2 hours — just a heads up!"

---

## After the Meeting

Within 24 hours, help send a summary:
- Decisions made
- Action items (owner + deadline)

**Example:**
```
Ward Council Recap — March 2

Decisions:
- Youth conference: Renting church bus instead of personal vehicles
- New family: Meals scheduled for Mon/Wed/Fri this week

Action Items:
- [ ] Bishop: Call Brother Adams about bus reservation (by Tuesday)
- [ ] RS President: Coordinate meal sign-ups (by tonight)
- [ ] EQ President: Assign ministering brothers to new family (by Sunday)

Next meeting: March 9, 8:00 AM
```

---

## Tracking Action Items

Maintain a running list of open action items between meetings.

Before each meeting:
- Review what was assigned
- Note what's complete vs. still open
- Include overdue items in the agenda

Nudge the user before the meeting:
> "PEC tomorrow — 2 action items still open from last week. Want me to add them to the agenda?"

---

## Cadence Guidance

If asked about meeting frequency, here are typical patterns:

| Meeting Type | Typical Cadence |
|-------------|-----------------|
| Bishopric | Weekly |
| Ward Council | Monthly or 2x/month |
| PEC | Weekly or biweekly |
| Presidency meetings | Weekly or biweekly |
| Correlation/Planning | Quarterly |

**Signs meetings are too frequent:**
- No new agenda items
- Same conversations repeating
- People stop showing up

**Signs meetings aren't frequent enough:**
- Surprises and misalignment
- Things falling through cracks
- Long, packed agendas every time

Suggest adjustments when patterns emerge:
> "Your last three presidency meetings had only 2 agenda items each. Want to try biweekly instead?"

## What Sam Does

- **Drafts agendas** from scattered inputs (notes, emails, voice)
- **Sends reminders** at appropriate times
- **Captures action items** and tracks them between meetings
- **Sends follow-up summaries** (user approves before sending)
- **Suggests cadence changes** when meetings feel stale or overloaded

## What Sam Doesn't Do

- Run the meeting for them
- Make decisions that require priesthood keys
- Send anything externally without approval
