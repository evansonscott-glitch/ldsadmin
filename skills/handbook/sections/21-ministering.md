# Chapter 21: Ministering

Source: General Handbook Chapter 21
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/21-ministering

## 21.1 Ministering Overview

Ministering is caring for others as the Savior would. It is:
- The responsibility of every member
- More about relationships than visits
- Focused on individuals' spiritual and temporal needs

### Purpose
- Help individuals strengthen their faith in Jesus Christ
- Help individuals and families prepare for and keep sacred covenants
- Help members become more self-reliant

## 21.2 Organizing Ministering

### Ministering Assignments
- Elders quorum and Relief Society presidencies make assignments
- Assignments should be made prayerfully
- Consider individuals' needs and ministering brothers/sisters' abilities
- Youth may be assigned as companions to adults

### Companionships
- Usually two people assigned together
- Can be flexible based on circumstances
- Couples may minister together

### Who Receives Ministering
- Every member household should have ministering brothers and sisters
- Focus extra attention on:
  - New members
  - Less-active members
  - Single adults
  - Families with special challenges

## 21.3 Ministering Interviews

### Purpose
- Discuss the well-being of individuals and families
- Identify needs and coordinate care
- Provide support to ministering brothers and sisters

### Frequency
- At least quarterly
- More often as needed

### Who Conducts
- **Elders Quorum:** EQ presidency members interview ministering brothers
- **Relief Society:** RS presidency members interview ministering sisters

### What to Discuss
- How are the individuals/families doing?
- What are their spiritual and temporal needs?
- What support do they need from the ward?
- How can leaders help the ministering companionship?

### Reporting
- Report to ward council on needs (maintaining confidentiality)
- Coordinate with bishopric on significant concerns

## 21.4 What Ministering Looks Like

### Not Just Visits
- Regular contact (in person, phone, text, etc.)
- Genuine friendship
- Service when needed
- Prayers for those you minister to

### Flexibility
- No required frequency of contact
- Adapt to individuals' needs and preferences
- Some may want frequent contact; others prefer less

### Focus Areas
- Building genuine relationships
- Helping with spiritual growth
- Responding to temporal needs
- Connecting people to ward resources

## 21.5 Coordinating Ministering

### Ward Council
- EQ and RS presidents report on ministering efforts
- Identify individuals/families needing extra support
- Coordinate assignments for those with special needs

### Coordination with Bishopric
- Significant needs reported to bishop
- Bishop may involve additional resources
- Maintain appropriate confidentiality

## Key Principles

> "The Savior showed us what it means to minister. He loved, taught, prayed for, comforted, and blessed those around Him."

> "Ministering is not about checking a box. It's about genuinely caring for God's children."

> "The quality of ministering matters more than the quantity of contacts."
