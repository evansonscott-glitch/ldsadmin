# Chapter 29: Meetings in the Church

Source: General Handbook Chapter 29
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/29-meetings-in-the-church

## 29.1 Purpose of Meetings

Church meetings help members:
- Worship God and partake of the sacrament
- Strengthen faith and testimony
- Learn and teach the gospel
- Conduct Church business
- Plan and coordinate God's work

### Key Principle
> "Meetings should focus on strengthening faith in Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ."

## 29.2 Ward Meetings

### 29.2.1 Sunday Meetings Schedule
Standard block (can be adjusted by stake presidency):
- **Sacrament meeting:** 60 minutes
- **Classes:** 50 minutes (alternating Sundays between Sunday School and quorum/organization meetings)

### 29.2.2 Sacrament Meeting
**Purpose:** Worship, partake of sacrament, strengthen faith

**Who Attends:** All ward members

**Who Conducts:** Member of the bishopric

**Typical Agenda:**
1. Prelude music
2. Welcome and announcements
3. Opening hymn and prayer
4. Ward business (sustainings, releases)
5. Sacrament hymn
6. Sacrament administration
7. Gospel messages (talks, testimonies)
8. Closing hymn and prayer

**Guidelines:**
- Focus on Jesus Christ and His Atonement
- Speakers should be assigned in advance
- Avoid travelogues; focus on gospel principles
- Fast and testimony meeting on first Sunday

### 29.2.3 Additional Sunday Meetings
- **Priesthood quorum meetings** (2nd and 4th Sundays)
- **Relief Society meetings** (2nd and 4th Sundays)
- **Young Women meetings** (2nd and 4th Sundays)
- **Primary** (every Sunday during class time)
- **Sunday School** (1st and 3rd Sundays)

### 29.2.4 Bishopric Meeting
**Purpose:** Plan, coordinate, address ward needs

**Who Attends:** Bishopric, clerk, executive secretary

**When:** Weekly

**Agenda Items:**
- Spiritual thought
- Review calendar and upcoming events
- Discuss individuals and families needing attention
- Plan sacrament meetings
- Review callings and assignments
- Address temporal and spiritual needs

### 29.2.5 Ward Council
**Purpose:** Plan God's work in the ward, address needs

**Who Attends:** Bishopric, EQ president, RS president, YW president, Primary president, Sunday School president, ward mission leader, ward temple and family history leader

**When:** Usually monthly; as needed

**Agenda Items:**
- Spiritual thought
- Review progress on ward goals
- Discuss individuals and families
- Coordinate ministering efforts
- Plan activities and programs
- Address ward challenges

### 29.2.6 Ward Youth Council
**Purpose:** Youth help plan activities, address youth needs

**Who Attends:** Bishopric member, YM/YW presidencies, class/quorum presidents

**When:** Monthly

### 29.2.7 Priesthood Executive Committee (PEC)
**Purpose:** Address confidential welfare and temporal needs

**Who Attends:** Bishopric, EQ president, RS president, ward clerk

**When:** As needed (often weekly or bi-weekly)

**Focus:**
- Confidential welfare matters
- Temporal needs
- Ministering coordination
- Temple recommend renewals

## 29.3 Stake Meetings

### Key Stake Meetings
- **Stake conference:** Twice yearly
- **Stake priesthood leadership meeting:** As needed
- **Stake council:** Monthly
- **High council:** Usually monthly

## 29.5 Guidelines for Planning Meetings

### Effective Meetings
1. **Start and end on time**
2. **Have a clear purpose** — if no purpose, don't meet
3. **Prepare an agenda** and share in advance
4. **Invite the Spirit** through prayer and focus
5. **Focus on people**, not just programs
6. **Make decisions and assign follow-up**
7. **Keep confidential matters confidential**

### Reducing Meeting Burden
- Combine meetings when possible
- Cancel meetings without sufficient agenda items
- Use technology for brief coordination
- Respect members' time

## Key Quote

> "Meetings should be kept to the minimum frequency and duration necessary to accomplish their purposes."
