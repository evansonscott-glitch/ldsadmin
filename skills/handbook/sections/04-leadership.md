# Chapter 4: Leadership in the Church of Jesus Christ

Source: General Handbook Chapter 4
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/4-leadership-in-the-church-of-jesus-christ

## 4.1 The Purpose of Leadership

Leaders help others become true followers of Jesus Christ. The purpose is to help Heavenly Father's children:
- Increase their faith in Jesus Christ and His Atonement
- Strengthen themselves and their families
- Find joy through keeping covenants

## 4.2 Principles of Christlike Leadership

### 4.2.1 Prepare Spiritually
- Seek personal revelation through prayer, scripture study, and temple attendance
- Live worthy of the Holy Ghost's guidance

### 4.2.2 Minister to All of God's Children
- See others as God sees them
- Reach out to those who need help
- Focus on individuals, not just programs

### 4.2.3 Teach the Gospel of Jesus Christ
- Teach pure doctrine
- Invite others to act on what they learn
- Bear testimony

### 4.2.4 Preside in Righteousness
- Lead with love, not authority
- Counsel with others before making decisions
- Follow proper channels

### 4.2.5 Delegate Responsibility and Ensure Accountability
- Trust others with meaningful assignments
- Provide necessary training and resources
- Follow up appropriately

### 4.2.6 Prepare Others to Be Leaders
- Help members develop leadership abilities
- Give stretch assignments
- Allow others to learn from experience

## 4.3 Councils in the Church

Councils are essential for:
- Receiving revelation
- Making wise decisions
- Unifying members in purpose

### Effective Council Practices
- Invite participation from all members
- Seek consensus, not just majority
- Focus on people, not just programs
- Maintain confidentiality

## 4.4 Coordinating God's Work

### Ward Level
- Bishopric coordinates all ward efforts
- Ward council aligns organizations
- PEC addresses temporal and spiritual needs

### Stake Level
- Stake presidency coordinates with bishops
- High council supports wards
- Stake council addresses stake-wide needs

## Key Quotes

> "Leadership in the Church is not about power or position. It is about serving God's children."

> "Effective leaders help others act in faith to fulfill their own responsibilities."

> "Councils provide a setting where leaders can seek revelation together."
