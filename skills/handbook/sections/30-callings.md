# Chapter 30: Callings in the Church

Source: General Handbook Chapter 30
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook/30-callings-in-the-church

## 30.1 Callings Overview

### Purpose of Callings
- Serve others
- Participate in God's work
- Grow spiritually through service
- Develop Christlike attributes

### Who May Hold Callings
- Must be baptized and confirmed members
- Must be willing to serve
- For callings requiring priesthood: must hold appropriate priesthood
- New members may receive callings soon after baptism (simple callings initially)

### Callings Requiring Temple Recommend
Certain callings require a current temple recommend:
- Bishopric
- Stake presidency
- Temple workers
- Seminary/Institute teachers
- And others as specified

## 30.2 Prayerful Consideration

Leaders should:
1. **Identify the need** — what does the calling require?
2. **Pray for guidance** — seek revelation on who should serve
3. **Consider individuals** — their circumstances, abilities, growth opportunities
4. **Consult with others** — bishopric, stake presidency as appropriate
5. **Receive confirmation** — feel peace about the decision

### Considerations
- Current callings and capacity
- Family circumstances
- Employment demands
- Health and abilities
- Growth opportunities

## 30.3 Extending a Calling

### Who Extends
- **Ward callings:** Bishopric member (or assigned leader)
- **Stake callings:** Stake presidency member (or assigned leader)

### Process
1. Interview the member
2. Determine worthiness if required
3. Explain the calling and its responsibilities
4. Ask if they're willing to serve
5. Allow time to consider and pray if needed
6. Express confidence and appreciation

### Key Principles
- Callings are invitations, not assignments
- Members should feel the Spirit's confirmation
- Never pressure or manipulate
- Be clear about expectations

## 30.4 Sustaining

### Purpose
- Ward members show support
- Member commits publicly to serve
- Allows objections to be raised (rare)

### Process
- Announce the calling in sacrament meeting (or appropriate meeting)
- Ask for sustaining vote
- Acknowledge the vote

## 30.5 Setting Apart

### Purpose
- Confer authority to act in the calling
- Pronounce blessings
- Officially begin service

### Who Sets Apart
- Must hold Melchizedek Priesthood
- Usually the leader who extended the calling
- Or a priesthood leader with authority over that calling

### Process
- Place hands on person's head
- State full name
- State the calling
- Set apart to serve
- Pronounce appropriate blessings
- Close in the name of Jesus Christ

## 30.6 Orientation and Training

### Responsibility
- Leaders should ensure new members in callings receive:
  - Clear understanding of responsibilities
  - Necessary training and resources
  - Ongoing support

### Resources
- Handbook sections for the calling
- Training from outgoing person
- Leadership training meetings
- Online resources (ChurchofJesusChrist.org)

## 30.7 Releasing from Callings

### When to Release
- Calling is no longer needed
- Person is called to another position
- Personal circumstances change
- Other appropriate reasons

### Process
1. Inform the person privately before public release
2. Express gratitude for service
3. Announce release and ask for vote of thanks in sacrament meeting

### Key Principle
> "Every member who is released should feel thanked and valued for their service."

## 30.8 Duration of Callings

- Most callings have no fixed term
- Leaders should prayerfully consider when changes are needed
- Some callings (like bishop) typically last around 5 years
- Youth callings may change with age transitions
