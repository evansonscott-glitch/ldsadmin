# Handbook Skill — General Handbook Reference

## Purpose

Help users navigate and understand the General Handbook of Instructions. Answer policy questions, find relevant sections, and explain procedures.

## Source

**General Handbook: Serving in The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints**
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/manual/general-handbook

## How to Use

When a user asks about Church policy, procedures, or "how do I...":

1. **Check the sections/ folder** for pre-loaded key content
2. **If not found**, fetch from the official handbook URL
3. **Quote or summarize** the relevant section
4. **Always cite** the chapter and section number


## Important Principles

1. **Don't make up policy** — if you can't find it, say so and suggest they ask their bishop or stake president

2. **Note when things require priesthood keys** — some decisions must be made by specific leaders

3. **Acknowledge local adaptation** — the handbook allows flexibility; local leaders may have specific guidance

4. **Recent updates** — the handbook is updated periodically; check the "Summary of Recent Updates" section for changes
