## Notes

- **Highest ROI callings:** EQ/RS presidents, bishopric members, clerks, exec secretaries — these are where most "work" happens
- **LCR skill is critical** — almost every leader uses it, but navigation is confusing
- **Ordinances skill helps everyone** — not just leaders, any priesthood holder needs this reference
- **Keep playbooks practical** — focus on workflows, not just responsibilities
